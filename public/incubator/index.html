FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who runs NGEN Incubator?+

It's a joint program from NGEN, the NextGen Entrepreneurship Network, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit backing student founders, and Expop.

How do the Zoom sessions work?+

There's one live session on Zoom every week for twelve weeks, following Expop's college accelerator curriculum: Ideation in weeks 1–2, Incorporation in week 3, the Buildathon in week 4, Customer Discovery in weeks 5–6, Go-to-Market in weeks 7–8, Sales & Pilots in weeks 9–10, Pitching in week 11 and Pitch Day in week 12.

Can I join from any school?+

Yes. The Public Track is open to college students across the US. At least one founder on the team needs to be currently enrolled.

Do we need to be incorporated before applying?+

No. You need to be incorporated or plan to be. The Idea phase walks every team through incorporation.

Does my startup have to be software?+

It has to include software. Your product can be more than an app, but software needs to be part of what you build, since the MVP phase is built on Base44.

How does the $150K finale work?+

Every team pitches live at the week 12 Pitch Demo. Top teams are evaluated by Honors Fund for investment of up to $150K. Being selected for review isn't a guarantee of funding.

Is there an in-person version?+

Yes, the Campus Track runs the same four phases as in-person workshops at about 3 schools per cohort, hosted by each school's entrepreneurship club. See the Campus Track.

We already have an existing incubator and/or startup education program. What do we do?+

Our NGEN Incubator team provides a wide range of flexibility for our campus partners and would be happy to adjust our curriculum to align with yours or adjust the incubator requirements for your cohort accordingly. Just reach out at info@ngennetwork.org and we can come up with a customized plan for you.

What does the club do?+

Your club names campus leads, recruits 20+ student teams, co-interviews applicants and hosts the four workshops on campus. NGEN and Expop provide the curriculum, the interview process, the Buildathon and the path to Honors Fund.

How many campuses are selected?+

About three per cohort.

How many teams should we recruit?+

We recommend 20+ teams per campus. There's no cap.

When are the workshops?+

There are four in-person workshops, in weeks 1, 5, 9 and 12, ending with Pitch Day in week 12. Your club sets the exact dates.

Who can join our campus cohort?+

The same teams as the Public Track: software startups with at least one founder currently enrolled at a US college or university, incorporated or planning to be, and able to attend every workshop.

How do students at our campus apply?+

They apply through a specialized form set up for your campus. Teams interview once with Expop and/or your campus directors, and accepted teams join the cohort at the first workshop.

How does the $150K investment work?+

Each campus running it hosts their own finale. Every team pitches live at the week 12 Pitch Demo. Top teams are evaluated by Honors Fund for investment of up to $150K. Being selected for review isn't a guarantee of funding.

What if our school isn't selected?+

Your students can still join the Public Track, which runs weekly on Zoom and is open to college founders across the US. See the Public Track.
