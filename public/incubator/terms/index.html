Article 3Program Structure and Attendance Commitment

3.1Program Structure. The Program is a twelve (12) week, non-credit, educational program comprising the Idea, MVP, Go-to-Market, and Pitch phases, delivered through (a) the Public Track, consisting of weekly live virtual Sessions, and (b) the Campus Track, consisting of in-person workshops hosted by participating campus organizations. The Organizers may modify the schedule, content, format, speakers, Partners, or location of any Session, or cancel or postpone any Session, at any time and without liability.

3.2Minimum Attendance Requirement. As a material condition of participation, each Team shall attend, through at least one (1) founder present for the substantial entirety of the Session, no fewer than four (4) of the twelve (12) scheduled Sessions of the Program (the “Minimum Attendance Requirement”). For the Campus Track, the Minimum Attendance Requirement shall mean attendance at all four (4) scheduled in-person workshops. Attendance shall be determined solely by the Organizers’ records, including Zoom participant logs, check-in sheets, or such other method as the Organizers may designate, and such records shall be conclusive. Satisfaction of the Minimum Attendance Requirement is a floor and not a recommendation; the Organizers expect Teams to attend every Session.

3.3Catch-Up Requirement. In the event a Team fails to attend two (2) consecutive Sessions, the Team shall use its best efforts to promptly schedule a catch-up call with the NGEN Incubator team, at a mutually convenient time, to review the Team’s progress and any material missed. Such catch-up call shall not count toward the Minimum Attendance Requirement. A Team’s failure to make such best efforts may be taken into account by the Organizers under Section 3.4.

3.4Consequences of Non-Attendance. A Team that fails to satisfy, or in the Organizers’ reasonable judgment cannot satisfy, the Minimum Attendance Requirement may, in the Organizers’ sole discretion, be (a) deemed ineligible to present at Pitch Day; (b) deemed ineligible for referral to Honors Fund or any other investor, Partner, or opportunity; (c) denied any certificate, credential, or Partner benefit associated with the Program; and/or (d) removed from the Program. The Organizers may, but are not obligated to, excuse an absence for documented emergency, illness, or academic conflict communicated in advance.

3.5Good-Faith Participation. Each Participant shall participate in good faith, complete assigned work within a reasonable time, and keep the Organizers reasonably informed of the Venture’s progress, including by responding to Program surveys and progress check-ins.

4.1Obligation to Incorporate. As a condition of participation, each Team that has not already done so shall cause the Venture to be duly organized as a legal entity under the laws of a state of the United States no later than April 30, 2027 (the “Incorporation Deadline”), and shall furnish the Organizers, upon request, with reasonable evidence thereof, such as a certificate of incorporation or formation.

4.2Sole Responsibility. The Team acknowledges that the choice of entity type, jurisdiction, capitalization, founder equity allocation, vesting, and intellectual-property assignment are matters within the Team’s sole responsibility. Any guidance, templates, or Partner services offered during the Program’s Incorporation Session are provided for general educational purposes only and do not constitute legal, tax, or accounting advice. The Team shall bear all filing fees, registered-agent fees, franchise taxes, and other costs of incorporation, and is strongly encouraged to consult independent counsel.

4.3Investor Requirements. The Team acknowledges that Honors Fund or other investors may require a particular entity type or jurisdiction (including, without limitation, a Delaware C-corporation) as a condition of investment, and that satisfaction of Section 4.1 does not ensure the Venture meets any such requirement.

4.4Failure to Incorporate. A Team that fails to incorporate by the Incorporation Deadline may be deemed ineligible for referral to Honors Fund or other investment opportunities and may be removed from the Program.

Campus Partner Terms and Conditions

Accepted on behalf of the student organization by the officer who submits the Partner Application.

Important notice

BY SUBMITTING AN APPLICATION TO BECOME A CAMPUS PARTNER OF THE NGEN INCUBATOR, OR BY HOSTING, PROMOTING, OR OTHERWISE PARTICIPATING IN THE CAMPUS TRACK IN ANY MANNER, THE STUDENT ORGANIZATION ON WHOSE BEHALF THE APPLICATION IS SUBMITTED, AND THE INDIVIDUAL SUBMITTING IT, ACKNOWLEDGE THAT THEY HAVE READ, UNDERSTOOD, AND ACCEPTED THESE TERMS AND CONDITIONS IN THEIR ENTIRETY. These Campus Partner Terms and Conditions (these “Terms”) constitute a legally binding agreement by and among NextGen Entrepreneurship Network, a not-for-profit organization registered in the State of New York (“NGEN”); Ascendance US Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as Expop (“Expop,” and together with NGEN, the “Organizers”); and the student organization identified in the Partner Application (the “Campus Partner”). The Organizers and the Campus Partner are each a “Party” and together the “Parties.”

Background. The Organizers operate the NGEN Incubator, a twelve (12) week educational program for student founders (the “Program”). The Program includes a Campus Track under which selected student organizations host in-person workshops for a cohort of student teams at their campus. These Terms govern the Campus Partner’s application to host, and its hosting of, the Campus Track at the college or university identified in the Partner Application (the “Host Institution”).

Article 1Definitions

As used herein: “Partner Application” means the online form through which a student organization applies to become a Campus Partner, together with all information submitted therein; “Campus Cohort” means the student teams admitted to the Campus Track at the Host Institution for the applicable cohort; “Campus Leads” means the officers or members of the Campus Partner designated under Section 3.1; “Participant” means any student founder participating in the Program; “Participant Terms” means the NGEN Incubator Participant Terms and Conditions, as amended from time to time; “Program Materials” means all curricula, facilitator guides, slides, recordings, templates, worksheets, frameworks, and other content furnished by or on behalf of the Organizers; and “Workshops” means the four (4) in-person Campus Track workshops described in Exhibit A.

Article 2Term

2.1Term. These Terms bind the Campus Partner upon submission of the Partner Application. The Campus Partner’s rights and obligations to host the Campus Track commence upon the Organizers’ written acceptance of the Partner Application (the “Acceptance Date”) and continue through the date that is thirty (30) days after the final Workshop of the cohort for which the Partner Application is accepted (the “Term”), unless earlier terminated under Article 15.

2.2Subsequent Cohorts. The Campus Partner’s participation in any subsequent cohort shall require a new Partner Application or the Parties’ confirmation by email that these Terms, as then in effect, apply to such cohort.

Article 3Campus Partner Responsibilities

3.1Campus Leads. The Campus Partner shall designate no fewer than two (2) Campus Leads, identified in the Partner Application, who shall serve as the Organizers’ primary points of contact, respond to Organizer communications within two (2) business days during the Term, and promptly notify the Organizers of any change in Campus Leads or in the Campus Partner’s officers.

3.2Recruitment. The Campus Partner shall use reasonable efforts to promote the Program to students at the Host Institution and to recruit a target of no fewer than twenty (20) applicant teams for the Campus Cohort. Such target is a goal and not a guarantee. All promotional materials shall be based on materials furnished or approved in writing by the Organizers, and the Campus Partner shall not make any statement concerning the Program, its outcomes, or any investment opportunity that is inconsistent with the Participant Terms.

3.3Applications and Selection. The Campus Partner shall administer applications for the Campus Cohort through the application form designated by the Organizers and shall recommend a Campus Cohort to the Organizers by the date designated by the Organizers in writing. The Organizers shall have final approval of the Campus Cohort. The Campus Partner shall apply the eligibility criteria set forth in the Participant Terms and shall not discriminate against any applicant on any basis prohibited by law or by the policies of the Host Institution.

3.4Participant Terms. The Campus Partner shall ensure that each member of the Campus Cohort accepts the Participant Terms through the Organizers’ application form before attending the first Workshop, and shall not admit to any Workshop any person who has not done so, other than invited guests, speakers, and Campus Partner members acting in a hosting capacity.

3.5Workshops. The Campus Partner shall (a) schedule the four (4) Workshops during the Program weeks set forth in Exhibit A and confirm the dates to the Organizers by the date designated by the Organizers in writing; (b) secure, at its own expense or through the Host Institution, a suitable venue for each Workshop with adequate capacity, internet access, and audiovisual equipment; (c) obtain all event registrations, approvals, and permits required by the Host Institution; and (d) host and facilitate each Workshop using the Program Materials and in accordance with the Organizers’ facilitator guides.

3.6Attendance Records. The Campus Partner shall record attendance at each Workshop using the method designated by the Organizers and deliver such records to the Organizers within three (3) business days after each Workshop. The Campus Partner shall promptly notify the Organizers of any team that misses a Workshop.

3.7Compliance with Institutional Policies. The Campus Partner shall conduct all Program activities in compliance with applicable law and with the policies of the Host Institution governing student organizations, events, facilities, recording, alcohol, safety, sponsorship, and use of institutional names and marks, and shall remain a recognized student organization in good standing throughout the Term.

3.8Incident Reporting. The Campus Partner shall notify the Organizers at info@ngennetwork.org within twenty-four (24) hours of becoming aware of any injury, safety incident, allegation of harassment or misconduct, property damage, or complaint arising in connection with any Workshop, and shall report such matter to the Host Institution where its policies so require.

Article 4Organizer Responsibilities

4.1Program Materials and Support. The Organizers shall provide the Campus Partner, without charge, with (a) the Program Materials necessary to deliver the Workshops; (b) facilitator guidance and a designated Organizer point of contact; (c) promotional templates and approved Program descriptions; and (d) access for the Campus Cohort to such Partner tools and accounts as are made available to the Public Track.

4.2Program Access. Members of the Campus Cohort shall be eligible for the Program’s weekly virtual sessions, Pitch Day, and referral for review by Honors Fund on the same terms and conditions as Participants in the Public Track, as set forth in the Participant Terms.

4.3Speakers. The Organizers may, but are not obligated to, arrange for speakers, mentors, or Organizer staff to attend Workshops in person or virtually.

Article 5Fees, Expenses, and Sponsorship

5.1No Fees. Unless otherwise agreed in a separate writing signed by the Parties, no fee is payable by either Party to the other under these Terms, and no Party shall charge any Participant a fee to apply to or participate in the Program.

5.2Expenses. Each Party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in performing these Terms. The Organizers shall not be responsible for any venue, catering, travel, printing, or other expense incurred by the Campus Partner unless approved in advance in writing.

5.3Sponsorship. The Campus Partner shall not solicit or accept sponsorship, donations, or in-kind support for the Program, or use the names of the Organizers, Honors Fund, or any Organizer partner in any sponsorship solicitation, without the Organizers’ prior written approval.
